# Literature Review

## Digital Twins

A digital twin is a virtual representation of a physical asset that is kept in step with it through data (Grieves & Vickers, 2017).
Most research on digital twins is technical, concerned with how to build them rather than how they are used (Smith & Jones, 2025).

## Sensemaking in Maintenance Work

Maintenance engineers make sense of equipment through a mix of instruments, experience and talk with colleagues (Orlikowski, 1992; Weick, 1995).
Sensemaking is triggered when expectations are violated, and it shapes what people notice next (Weick, 1995, p. 43).
