**Abstract.**
Digital twins promise to change how organisations maintain complex equipment, yet little is known about how the people doing the work come to rely on them.
Drawing on {{n_interviews}} with maintenance engineers at {{n_sites}}, we show how digital twins shift maintenance work from repairing failures to anticipating them.
We contribute a process model of anticipatory maintenance and discuss what it means for the design of digital twins.

_Keywords:_ digital twins, maintenance, sensemaking, qualitative research
