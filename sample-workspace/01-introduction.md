# Introduction

Organisations increasingly pair physical equipment with a digital twin: a live software model fed by sensors (Smith & Jones, 2025).
The promise is that problems can be seen before they happen.
Yet studies of technology at work have long shown that new tools change practice in ways their designers did not foresee (Orlikowski, 1992).
