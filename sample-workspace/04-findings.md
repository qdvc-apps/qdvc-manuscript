# Findings

Engineers first treated the digital twin as a second opinion, checking it against what they could see and hear.
Over time, the twin came to set the agenda: engineers went looking for problems the twin had flagged, as one senior engineer explained.

> At first I only looked at the screen when something had already gone wrong.
> Now the screen tells me where to go in the morning.

Three practices marked this shift:

- reading trends rather than alarms;
- scheduling work by predicted rather than actual failure;
- arguing about the model, not only the machine.
